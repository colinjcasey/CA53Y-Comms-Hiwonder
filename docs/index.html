HIWONDER S3

Turns the Hiwonder ESP32-S3 Development Board into a CA53Y messenger and repeater with a touchscreen, a clock, your cameras, and hold-to-talk. Flash it from this page.

Flash your board

Plug the board into this computer with a USB-C data cable, pick your network, then click Install. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop; Safari and Firefox can't talk to USB serial devices.

Standard and Long Range devices can't hear each other. Pick the same network as the messengers and relays in your group.

Hiwonder ESP32-S3 Development Board

The AI Vision & Smart Chat board with the 2.0 inch touchscreen, speaker and microphone. Replaces Hiwonder's AI firmware; you can flash theirs back any time.

This browser can't flash boards. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure page (https).

No port shows up? Try another cable (many are charge-only). If it still won't connect, hold BOOT while you click Install.

After it boots

  1. Type your name. It's shown to people you message.
  2. Tap Chats, then + to add someone by their device ID, or Nearby to see who's in range.
  3. In a chat, tap Type to use the keyboard, or hold Hold to talk and speak.
  4. New messages fill the screen. Tap Play to hear them read aloud, or Reply to answer.
  5. For the clock and cameras, open Settings, then Wi-Fi, and join your network.

Choosing "Erase device" during install wipes your name, contacts, Wi-Fi and cameras. Leave it unchecked when you're only updating. Updates keep your settings, so you can also switch between Standard and Long Range without setting the board up again. Settings, About shows which network it's on.

Wi-Fi, cameras and voice

Nothing is built in: you enter your own network, cameras and speech server, on the board or from your phone at the address shown under Settings, then Web settings.

The CA53Y mesh runs on Wi-Fi channel 1. If your router is also on channel 1, the board stays on Wi-Fi and keeps relaying messages. On any other channel it joins Wi-Fi only while it needs it, for the clock, a camera on screen, or turning speech into text, and the status bar shows when the mesh is paused.

On Long Range, mesh messages always go out in Long Range mode, and Wi-Fi to your router still works normally.

Hold to talk sends your recording to a speech-to-text server you choose (OpenAI, or a Whisper server on your own network), then shows you the text before you send it. Without Wi-Fi, use the keyboard.
